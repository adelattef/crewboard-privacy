Crew Board — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Crew Board (the “app”) is a roster and crew-community app for airline crew — pilots and cabin crew. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and your choices. The app is operated by Adel Attef (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at any time at copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.

The guiding principle of Crew Board is data minimisation: the information the app needs to do its job stays on your device wherever possible, and we only store on our servers the small amount of data required for your account and the roster-sharing features.

1. Your crew-system (eCrew / AIMS) login stays on your device

To show your schedule, the app connects to your airline’s crew system (eCrew / AIMS) on your behalf. Your crew-system username and password are stored only on your device, in the device’s protected local storage. They are sent directly from your device to your airline’s servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to log you in. They are never transmitted to, processed by, or stored on our servers, and we never have access to them.

For airlines that require an interactive sign-in (for example, two-factor authentication with an authenticator app), the app displays your airline’s own login page inside the app. You sign in directly with the airline; the app then uses the resulting session, on your device only, to fetch your roster. Codes you enter go to your airline, never to us.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is kept locally on your device and is not uploaded to us:

  • Your roster / schedule and duty details retrieved from the crew system, including flight details, layover hotel information, and the crew names / photos your airline’s system provides for your duties.
  • Your profile details: airline, role, and home base.
  • Personal day memos and app settings (for example, theme and time-display preferences).

If you sign out, uninstall the app, or clear its data, this on-device information is removed.

3. Data we store for your account and roster sharing

Crew Board uses an account so you can connect with colleagues and share rosters. The backend is provided by Supabase (see “Service providers” below). The same account works in our companion app CoPilot — one account across both apps. We store:

  • Your account email address and password (the password is handled and securely hashed by Supabase Auth — we never see it in plain text).
  • Your chosen display name, your role (pilot / cabin crew), and a generated friend code.
  • Your friend relationships (who you have connected with).
  • Roster summaries you choose to share: your duty days for a month, and per-day details such as report/debrief times, scheduled flight legs, and layover hotel — so accepted friends can see your availability.

Only friends you have accepted can view your shared roster. Access is enforced at the database level so that no other user can read your data.

We deliberately do not share or store on our servers:

  • Your crew-system credentials or sessions (see section 1).
  • Personal memos, or the crew lists / crew photos of your duties.

4. Emails we send

We send transactional emails only — for example, a password-reset code when you request one. These are sent from copilotpilotapp@gmail.com. We do not send marketing emails.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not use advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your device location.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

6. Service providers

We use Supabase (supabase.com) to host the database and authentication for accounts and roster sharing. Supabase processes the data described in section 3 on our behalf. We use Google Play and Apple App Store / TestFlight to distribute the app. Transactional email is delivered via Google’s mail servers. These providers process data only as needed to provide their service.

7. Data retention and deletion

On-device data exists only on your device and is removed when you sign out, uninstall the app, or clear its data. Account and sharing data is retained until you delete your account. To delete your account and all associated server-side data, email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com from the address on your account — see the account deletion page for details. Because one account serves both Crew Board and CoPilot, deleting it removes your account from both apps. Removing a friend deletes that sharing relationship for both sides.

8. Security

  • Crew-system credentials never leave your device and are never sent to us.
  • All network connections use encryption (HTTPS / TLS).
  • Account and sharing data is protected by per-user database access rules so you can only read your own data and data shared with you by accepted friends.

9. Children

Crew Board is a professional tool intended for working airline crew and is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.

Crew Board is an independent app and is not endorsed by or affiliated with any airline. Airline and crew-system names are used only to describe interoperability.
