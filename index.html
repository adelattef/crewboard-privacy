Crew Board — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Crew Board (the “app”) is published by AAB@safespace (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have. Contact: copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.

1. Information on your device

Most of what the app does happens on your device. Information you enter or keep in the app — your profile, schedule, day memos and settings — stays on your device unless you share it as described below. We do not receive it.

2. Account information

When you create an account, our authentication provider handles your email address and password; we never see your password. We also keep your display name, your role (pilot or cabin crew), a generated friend code, an account identifier, and the connections you make with other users. The same account is used by our companion app CoPilot.

3. Sharing with friends

If you choose to share your schedule with friends, the schedule details you share (dates, duties, times and layover details) are stored on our service so that the friends you have accepted can view them. Only accepted friends can see them. Removing a friend stops sharing with that person.

4. Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment. A subscription-management service receives your account identifier and purchase status so that paid features can be verified.

5. How information is used and shared

We use information only to operate accounts, connect friends, share schedules, count visits at partner venues, manage purchases, answer support requests and keep the app working. Our authentication, subscription-management and email-delivery providers, Apple and Google receive only what their service needs and must protect it in line with this policy. We send account emails only (verification, password reset); no marketing.

If you confirm a visit at a partner venue, we record that venue, the date and time, and your account, so we can tell the venue how many visits its own staff confirmed. The venue never sees who visited.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking.
  • No location collection.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Account information is kept until you delete your account. Visit records at partner venues are kept until you delete your account; venues receive only the number of visits, never who made them. Shared schedule details are replaced as you update them and removed when you stop sharing or delete the account. Purchase records may be kept by Apple, Google or the subscription-management service as their rules require.

To delete your account, open Profile in the app and choose Delete account, or email copilotpilotapp@gmail.com from your registered address — see the account deletion page. Deletion removes the account from both Crew Board and CoPilot, including any Swap Board records saved through CoPilot. Information kept only on your device is removed by uninstalling the app.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information.

9. Children

Crew Board is intended for working airline crew aged 18 or over.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes and will update the date above. Questions: copilotpilotapp@gmail.com.
